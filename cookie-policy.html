Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the owners of the site.

How We Use Cookies

Online Brain Hub uses cookies to:

  • Remember your login credentials and saved XP progress locally on your device.
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.
  • Analyze our website traffic to improve user experience.

Third-Party Cookies

In some special cases, we also use cookies provided by trusted third parties, such as Google AdSense. These third-party advertisers may use cookies to track your web usage to serve you targeted ads. We have no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

Managing Cookies

You can prevent the setting of cookies by adjusting the settings on your browser (see your browser Help for how to do this). Be aware that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites that you visit, including your ability to save your quiz progress automatically.
